Ajudoku

The road to Kunju

Story

Aju met Kunju in 2015. Every year since has been another step toward their future together. Clear each year’s puzzle — from 2015 through 2024 — and help Aju marry Kunju.

Levels

    How to play

    1. Tap a cell to cycle: empty → ✕ → Aju.
    2. Place exactly one Aju in every row, every column, and every colored region.
    3. Ajus must not touch — not even diagonally.
    4. Use ✕ marks to track impossible tiles. Hint reveals one correct Aju.
    5. You have 3 hearts each year. You lose a heart whenever you put Aju in the wrong cell; lose all three and the year resets.
